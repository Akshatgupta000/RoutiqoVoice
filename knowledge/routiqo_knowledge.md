## What Routiqo is

Routiqo is a fleet dispatch and route optimization platform designed for last-mile logistics businesses. It helps a team manage delivery centers, vehicles, orders, dispatch operations, and optimized delivery routes from one operational dashboard.

Routiqo is a software product for planning and monitoring delivery work. This sales assistant can explain the product and record a demonstration request. It does not place telephone calls, and it does not book a calendar appointment.

## Fleet and vehicle management

Routiqo includes fleet and vehicle management. A dispatcher can keep a record of the vehicles used for delivery work and track vehicle capacity. Capacity matters because an order should not be assigned to a vehicle that cannot carry it.

## Delivery center management

Routiqo includes delivery center management. Delivery centers are the locations from which orders leave for customers. The platform is built for businesses that run last-mile deliveries from these centers.

## Order management

Routiqo includes order management. Orders are the deliveries that need to be planned. The platform supports order assignment, so delivery work can be connected to a vehicle instead of being tracked only in a separate spreadsheet or a phone call.

## Dispatch and route planning

Routiqo provides a fleet dispatch dashboard, route generation, and route optimization. Dispatchers can plan delivery work, inspect a route, and follow delivery status. Priority-based delivery planning is part of the product, so more urgent orders can be planned ahead of lower-priority work.

The dashboard is an operations screen for dispatch. It is not a telephone dialer and it is not a CRM.

## Monitoring and analytics

Routiqo includes delivery status monitoring, route inspection, and operational analytics. These let a team see how planned deliveries are progressing and review route and dispatch activity. The knowledge base does not publish benchmark numbers for those analytics.

## How routing works

Routiqo uses a combination of spatial assignment and route optimization techniques:

- Voronoi-based service zone partitioning divides a service area into zones so nearby work stays associated with the closest zone.
- Capacity-aware vehicle clustering groups orders while respecting how much a vehicle can carry.
- Greedy nearest-neighbor route construction builds a route by repeatedly choosing a nearby remaining stop. It is a straightforward construction method.
- 2-opt route improvement adjusts parts of a route when doing so shortens it.
- OSRM road network routing follows the road network rather than drawing a straight line between stops.

These are the routing techniques this knowledge base confirms. Do not claim extra solvers, live traffic guarantees, or autonomous driving.
